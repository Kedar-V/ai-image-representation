Mirror or window?

Ask an AI tool for a picture of a nurse and it has to pick a person. Should that picture match who does the job today, or show who could? I asked a free image model for 60 pictures to see what it picks.

    What the results suggest

    • If the plain prompt shows fewer women than actually work in a job, the model is exaggerating a pattern that already exists.
    • If the rewrite works, it pushes the picture toward balance. That might be what you want for "a CEO" in a career brochure for kids.
    • The same rewrite fails on history. The Mercury Seven were all men, and a tool that adds "gender-balanced" to every prompt ignores what the user asked for.
    • Richter et al. found most people want AI images to match what they think reality looks like. That sense of reality comes partly from the images people see. Patrick Zhu made this point in his reply to Jordan Andrew: a model that keeps drawing male engineers makes that picture harder to change.
    • So someone has to choose the goal before choosing a fix: an accurate record, a neutral default, or a picture of what could be. The right choice depends on the prompt and on who is asking.
